# Physics Bass Roadmap

This roadmap tracks the physical bass engine, playable editor, and plugin integration.

## Milestone 1: Physical Bass Engine — COMPLETED

- CFL-bounded FDTD stiff-string model with geometric tension modulation and bounded fret contact.
- Finger, pick, and slap excitation, including support for the low B string.
- Electric pickup aperture and tone filtering, plus acoustic A0/B1/bridge-hill body modes.

## Milestone 2: Playable Editor and Plugin — COMPLETED

- Vizia editor with an interactive five-string, 24-fret fretboard and MIDI audition.
- Physical parameter racks and preset selection.
- CLAP, VST3, and standalone plugin adapter.
